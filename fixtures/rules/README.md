# Rules fixtures

One JSON file per rules case for Mini Xiangqi, Xiangqi and Jieqi: a position, an optional move line, and what the core's rules facade must answer there. The expected values come from the rules in `docs/xiangqi-rules.md` and `docs/jieqi-rules.md`, not from an engine's output. Gomoku and Renju have none, because their pinned engine is their rules authority. A fixture's meaning never changes once accepted; a corrected reading is a new fixture.

Ids are `<game>-<area>-NNN`, with game `mx`, `xq` or `jq` agreeing with `variant`, and area one of `move` (legality), `end` (checkmate and stalemate), `cnt` (move-count draw), `rep` (repetition), `chk` (perpetual check), `chs` (perpetual chase), `mix` (mixed and mutual violations) or `set` (setup legality).

Every file has `id`, `title`, `area`, `variant` (`minixiangqi`, `xiangqi` or `jieqi`), `start_fen` (six fields; a face-down Jieqi piece is its identity letter followed by `~`) and `rationale`. Then it has either:

- `setup`, for a setup case: `status` (`ok`, `illegal-position` or `invalid-fen`), `rule` (`piece-count`, `palace`, `elephant-side`, `soldier-rank`, `facing-generals`, `opponent-in-check`, `not-frozen-start` or `not-dealt-start`), `side` and `square`, each `null` where it does not apply; or
- `moves`, `assertions` and `boundary`, for a play case. `moves` are coordinate moves such as `b1b4` or `a9a10`. `assertions` holds `in_check`, `result_fen`, `legal_moves`, `rejected_moves`, `applied` (single-move probes, each with `move`, `result_fen` and `in_check`) and `game_state`: `state` is `ongoing`, `claimable-draw`, `red-wins`, `black-wins` or `draw`; `reason` is `null` or one of `checkmate`, `stalemate`, `threefold-repetition`, `perpetual-check`, `perpetual-chase`, `mutual-perpetual-check`, `mutual-perpetual-chase`, `fifty-move-rule` or `forty-move-rule`; `at_occurrence` is the repetition count the outcome attaches at, or `0`. `boundary`, when present, gives a `prefix_len` one repetition cycle earlier at which the outcome must not yet hold, with an `expect` note.
